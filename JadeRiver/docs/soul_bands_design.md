# P8 · Soul Bands — beast souls worn as rings of light (design and build brief)

This page is both the design of Jade River's Soul Bands and the brief a builder (human or agent) follows to build them
in P8b, inside v1.3. It carries the soul-ring system of the Soul Land novels over mechanic for mechanic into Jade
River's world and names, as `docs/roadmap_master_ui.md` asks (row P8, decision 2, conflict C5). The research behind it,
with sources and confidence labels, is `docs/research/soul_band_research.md`. The novels are the reference for
mechanics only: no name, term, colour order, text or art of theirs appears in the game.

---

## 0. The brief

**Role.** You are building one system of a Godot 4 offline-first game whose simulation is split into pure rules
(`scripts/simulation/rules/`) and authorities that own state and emit events (`scripts/simulation/authority/`), with
all content generated from `tools/data/*.py` into `data/*.json`.

**Goal.** From Spirit Awakening 1, when the Soul bar appears, a cultivator can take the soul of a beast they have slain
with their own hand and wear it as a **Soul Band**: a ring of light about the waist in the colour of the beast's rank.
Each band gives one **beast art**, a technique drawn from the beast's kind and element and paid for in Soul. Realms
open more band slots, nine in all. A band from a beast of a higher realm than one's own is stronger and can be worn,
but it risks Qi Deviation, a torn meridian and, at worst, a Band Burst. The player's game gains a hunt with a purpose:
choose a beast, hunt it, decide whether to wear, bottle or release what it leaves, and build a set of arts around it.

**Definition of done (P8b):**

1. Data in `tools/data/bands.py`, generated by `build_data.py` into `data/bands.json`, with the art rows appended to
   `data/techniques.json`; validated by `data_validation`.
2. Pure formulas in `BandRules`; state and intents in `FieldAuthority`; every new event in `contract.py`.
3. UI reachable on a phone: the Cultivation page's Bands tab and the band ring on the ascent, the Wear dialog, the
   Codex's Bands tab, the Techniques page's band arts, the world's band light and rings.
4. `rules_tests` `band_suite` (the slots, the limit and the risk, the arts, the paths), a save round-trip,
   `valley_run` winning a first band, `balance_sim` checks of §8.
5. Full test suite green, `docs/CHANGELOG.md` entry, commit and push.

**Constraints.**

- Names are Jade River's own (§1). Never a Soul Land term in data, strings, code comments or art.
- Authorities write only their own state and emit only their own events. Randomness goes through `Rng` (the `field`
  stream for lights and wearing), so a replay with the same seed wins and wears the same bands.
- No new body animation and no new equipment (AGENTS.md). Wearing uses the existing meditation pose; an art uses its
  weapon family's first combo action; the beast is drawn from its own creature sheet (§6.7).
- No new HUD button (conflict C14). Arts go in the existing technique slots; the band light uses the context button.
- A failure hurts but never kills or deletes progress beyond the band itself (the tribulation's precedent, S48).

**Order of work:** data → rules → authority and the small hooks in other authorities → UI → tests → docs.

## 2. The loop

1. **Seek.** On the Cultivation page's Bands tab, choose a Sought Beast: any band-eligible species this character has
   slain, or one with a Bestiary Leaf on the account. The tab says where it lives and what its art would be.
2. **Hunt.** Every killing blow you land on the Sought Beast has a chance to leave its **Band Light**: a ring of light
   in the beast's rank colour, hanging where it fell. Beast Kings and field bosses always leave one to the one who
   fells them.
3. **Choose.** At the light the context button says *Wear*. The Wear dialog shows the band, your Band Limit, the risk
   and the supports, and offers **Wear**, **Bottle** (keep it in a gourd for later) or **Release** (let it go, for
   merit). Left alone, the light fades after two minutes or when you leave the room.
4. **Wear.** Wearing is a seated channel of 8 to 24 seconds that spends half the Soul bar. A blow breaks it. Within
   your limit it always holds; over it, the dialog's roll decides.
5. **Fight with it.** The band's art appears in the Techniques page's list; put it in a technique slot like any
   technique. It costs Soul, not QI. Every band worn also quickens Soul regeneration.
6. **Grow.** New realms open more slots. When the slots are full, a new band replaces an old one (which scatters, or
   goes into a gourd). Better bands come from higher-rank beasts, from going over the limit, and from Kings.

## 3. Rules (BandRules)

All constants live in `data/bands.json` under `rules`; the values here are the defaults.

### 3.1 Slots by realm

| From | Slots |
|---|---|
| Spirit Awakening 1 | 1 |
| Spirit Awakening 5 | 2 |
| Heaven Glimpse 1 | 3 |
| Sage 1 | 4 |
| Sage Sovereign 1 | 5 |
| Will Manifest 1 | 6 |
| Sphere Lord 1 | 7 |
| Law Touching 1 | 8 (v1.3) |
| Monarch 1 | 9 (v1.4) |

`slots_for(realm_key)` is the count of the last row the realm has reached (`ProgressionRules.at_least`), 0 before
Spirit Awakening 1 or without the `soul_bands` unlock. Slots never close. The first two come close together (about six
hours of play apart at the pacing in `balance.json`: Spirit Awakening 1 at 42 h, Heaven Glimpse 1 at 55 h), then one a
great realm, as the source's rings come fast early and slowly later.

*Worked example.* A Spirit Awakening 3 character has 1 slot; at Spirit Awakening 5, 2; a Sphere Lord 3 character, 7.

### 3.2 A beast's rank, realm and colour

- **Rank** (1–9) is `WorldAuthority.beast_rank(def, level)`, from the beast's Level at the kill: Levels 1–9 are rank 1,
  10–18 rank 2 … 73 and above rank 9. Rank sets the band's colour and its art's strength (§3.8). A species whose Level
  band crosses a boundary gives bands of either rank.
- **Realm** is the beast's great realm on the player's own ladder, `ProgressionRules.realm_index_for_level(level)` (the
  `EnemyState.realm_index` the enemy badge already uses). Up to rank 9 it equals the rank; past Level 81 it keeps
  climbing (Will Manifest 10, Sphere Lord 11, Law Touching 12), so the risk keeps meaning when the rank stops at 9.
- A **Crowned Band** counts one realm higher (§3.4).

| Rank | Levels | Band | Colour | Grade it echoes | Art strength (§3.8) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–9 | Ash | `#b9b2a0` | plain | ×1.00 |
| 2 | 10–18 | Pearl | `#e8e1cf` | common | ×1.12 |
| 3 | 19–27 | Moss | `#489655` | earth (deep) | ×1.25 |
| 4 | 28–36 | Leaf | `#67d67a` | earth | ×1.40 |
| 5 | 37–45 | Lake | `#4e81a8` | heaven (deep) | ×1.57 |
| 6 | 46–54 | Sky | `#6fb8f0` | heaven | ×1.76 |
| 7 | 55–63 | Violet | `#b07ce8` | mystic | ×1.97 |
| 8 | 64–72 | Frost | `#5ee0e8` | spirit | ×2.21 |
| 9 | 73 and up | Gold | `#d8c27a` | sage | ×2.48 |

**Why this palette.** A band takes the colour of the gear its beast's Level drops: the grade band (`stats.json`
`grade_bands`) of the rank's first Level, with the colours from `grades.json` `grade_colors`. Players already read
those colours on every drop and in the Codex, so a Sky band and heaven-grade gear come from the same hunting grounds,
and a higher band reads as better without a legend. Where two ranks share a grade (earth: 3 and 4; heaven: 5 and 6), the
lower rank takes the grade's colour at 70% value, so all nine differ. The order is not the source's (no black, no red):
red stays the game's danger colour (`UiKit.RED`, the red enemy badge), which a prized band must not look like. A
Crowned Band keeps its rank colour with a `UiKit.PALE_GOLD` rim.

### 3.3 Which kills leave a Band Light

A kill can leave a light only when all of these hold:

- the character has the `soul_bands` unlock;
- the victim is a **beast** (`beast_rank > 0`) of nature `spirit` or `demonic` (not `hollowed`) whose element does not
  begin with `hollow`: the Hollow has eaten the soul it would give;
- its role is not `event`, `story_boss` or `trial`, and it was not `summoned` by a boss;
- the killing blow was the character's own (attack, technique, art, treasure, talisman or their own damage over time),
  not a pet's, a companion's or the swarm's (`actor_defeated` gains `by`, §6.3);
- it was not taken whole by the Taming Cauldron (`captured` in the payload).

Then the chance is:

| Case | Chance |
|---|---|
| The Sought Beast, normal | 5% |
| The Sought Beast, elite | 25% |
| A Beast King or a field or dungeon boss (sought or not) | 100% |
| An elite or boss under Soul Search that dies within its window (sought or not; S48) | 100% |
| The Sought Beast dying while rooted by Rite-Seal Script (the Confucian path) | 100% |
| Any other kill | 0 |

The roll uses the `field` stream. A light holds its beast's species, Level, rank, realm, crowned and blood flags, and
hangs for **120 s** where the beast fell; it fades early when its character leaves the room. It is its killer's alone.
Several lights may hang at once.

*Worked example.* Hunting Mist Wolves at the balance sheet's 6 kills a minute, a light comes on average every
1 / (6 × 0.05) = 3.3 minutes of fighting, and half the time within 2.3 minutes.

### 3.4 The Band Limit and "over"

```
limit = realm_index(character's realm)            Spirit Awakening 6, Heaven Glimpse 7, Sage 8 … Sphere Lord 11
      + 1 with Gold Body (S48 body ladder)
beast_realm = realm_index_for_level(level at the kill) + (1 if crowned)
over  = beast_realm − limit                        at wearing time, with the realm then
```

The limit reads the character's true realm, never a false realm (S48 Concealment). A band bottled at Heaven Glimpse and
worn at Sage is judged at Sage.

### 3.5 Risk

```
over ≤ 0   certain: no roll, no supports used
over ≥ 4   refused: "It would burst you."
otherwise  index = clamp((over + 1) + unstable + injuries + heart_steps − min(2, supports), 0, 3)
           word  = low · moderate · high · severe       chance = 95% · 75% · 50% · 25% (curves.json risk_success)
```

`index` is `ProgressionRules.risk_index(over + 1, unstable, injuries, min(2, supports), false, heart_steps)`, the same
count a major breakthrough uses: `unstable` is Unstable stability, `injuries` the count of untreated injuries,
`heart_steps` one per 25 heart demon (`ProgressionRules.heart_demon_steps`).

**Supports** (each is one step; at most two count; the dialog shows all and marks the two used):

| Support | Condition | Spent? |
|---|---|---|
| A beast core | of the band's element (its parent element, as pet cores match) and a tier that covers the band's rank or more: low 2–3, mid 4–5, high 6–7, peak 8–9; any core of the element for rank 1 | yes, at the start |
| A second such core | as above | yes |
| The Soul Dao | tier 2 or more | no |
| Bestiary Leaves | the species' leaf tier 2 or more (5 leaves) | no |
| A bottleneck | the character stands at a sub-level's bottleneck (`state == "bottleneck"`) | no |
| A retreat room | the room has `retreat` (as breakthroughs) | no |
| Blood essence | on the Blood path, a full meter (100) | yes, emptied |

*Worked examples.*

- A Spirit Awakening 1 character (limit 6) wears a Mist Wolf's band (Level 48, realm 6): over 0, certain.
- The same character wears a Cloudpeak Roc's band (Level 60, realm 7): over 1, index 2, **high (50%)**. With a High
  Wind Core in the bag (one step) it is moderate (75%); with Soul Dao tier 2 as well, low (95%). With a body injury on
  top it is moderate again.
- A Heaven Glimpse 1 character (limit 7) wears a Thunderhorn Rhino's band (Level 66, realm 8): high (50%) bare; a Peak
  Thunder Core and a bottleneck make it low (95%).
- The same character offered a Void Crab's band (Level 96, realm 11): over 4, refused.
- A Sphere Lord 3 character (limit 11) wears the Nebula Leviathan's crowned band (Level 99, realm 11 + 1 = 12): over 1,
  high (50%); two Peak Space Cores make it low (95%).

### 3.6 Wearing

- Wearing starts from a light (in reach, the context button) or from a bottled band (the Bands tab), only when the
  character is not in a tribulation, a breakthrough channel or a wearing already, and has at least half its Soul.
- It spends **half of max Soul** at the start and any cores used, then runs a channel of **6 + 2 × rank seconds**
  (rank 6: 18 s; rank 9: 24 s) in the meditation pose. A blow landed on the character, a step off the spot, leaving the
  room or a grave wound breaks it (`band_wearing_broken`). The light stays until its own time runs out; the Soul and
  cores are not returned.
- At the end, a certain wearing succeeds; otherwise the roll (the `field` stream) against the chance of §3.5.
- **Success:** the band goes into the chosen slot (the first empty one, or the one the dialog named, §3.9), the light
  goes out, stats are rebuilt, and its art is known at once.

### 3.7 Failure

| What | When |
|---|---|
| **Qi Deviation** for 600 s (the existing S48 status: every technique and art takes a random element) | every failure |
| A **meridian tear** of severity = over (1–3; `injuries.json` `meridian`: −25% QI regeneration and +10% technique cost per step) | every failure |
| **Band Burst**: HP falls to a tenth of max, and a **soul injury** of severity 1 (−10% max Soul) | a failure at Severe (index 3) |
| The band is lost; the light goes out | every failure |

Nothing else is lost: no realm progress, no death. The Codex gains `band_burst` at the first burst.

*Worked example.* The Heaven Glimpse 1 character above wears the Rhino's band with only the Peak Thunder Core
(moderate, 75%) and fails: ten minutes of Qi Deviation, a severity-1 meridian tear (heals in 10 minutes, or with a
Qi Restoration Pill), the core and 195 Soul spent, the band gone. Had the index been Severe, HP would also have fallen to
a tenth and the Soul pool shrunk by a tenth until the soul injury heals.

### 3.8 Beast arts

Each band grants one art: a technique row generated per species (§5), known while the band is worn. Its **shape** comes
from the beast's kind, its **rider** from the beast's element, its **strength** from the band's rank, and its **size**
from the character (the art strikes with `soul_attack`, which follows Spirit and Insight, S48).

**Kinds** (the base before the rank's strength; `mult` is per hit):

| Kind | Shape | mult | Hits | Targets | Reach | Cooldown | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fang | a lunge and a double bite | 0.45–0.55 | 2 | 1 | 140 | 9 s | `dash` 120 |
| Claw | three rakes | 0.3–0.37 | 3 | 1 | 90 | 9 s | — |
| Talon | a stoop onto the first foe ahead and those beside it | 0.75–0.9 | 1 | 3 | 220 | 11 s | `knockup_s` 0.3 |
| Horn | a charge through foes | 0.75–0.9 | 1 | 4 | 200 | 12 s | `dash` 180, heavy knockback |
| Coil | binds one foe | 0.8–1.0 | 1 | 1 | 260 | 12 s | root 1.5 s (certain) |
| Shell | a ward, then a pinch | 0.55–0.7 | 1 | 1 | 90 | 16 s | shield of 15% max Soul for 5 s (`shield_soul_pct`, as Soul Lantern Ward) |
| Sting | a thrust whose rider always lands | 0.75–0.9 | 1 | 1 | 150 | 10 s | rider chance 100% |
| Wing | a gust wave ahead | 0.55–0.7 | 1 | 6 | 260 | 11 s | knockback 80 |
| Hurl | a thrown stone, bamboo or ice | 0.9–1.1 | 1 | 1 | 380 | 8 s | `projectile` (speed 700) |
| Tongue | pulls one foe in | 0.8–1.0 | 1 | 1 | 240 | 10 s | pull 120 (the combo pull) |
| Tail | a sweep on both sides | 0.5–0.65 | 1 | 8 | 150 | 11 s | `both_sides` |
| Maw | a burst from below ahead | 0.65–0.8 | 1 | 5 | 200 | 13 s | `knockup_s` 0.4 |

**Riders** by the art's element:

| Element | Rider |
|---|---|
| water | slow 25% for 2 s |
| wood | poison 2% of max HP a second for 4 s (the art counts as a poison art, §4.4) |
| fire | burn 2% a second for 4 s |
| earth | stun 0.5 s |
| metal | bleed 2% a second for 4 s |
| wind | knockback 60 |
| thunder | shock 0.4 s |
| star | confusion 1.5 s |
| space | no status; ignores 15% of resistance (`ignore_resistance`) every time |
| none | none |

A species may override its rider in the data (the Sandstorm Scorpion's earth sting poisons).

**Formulas:**

```
strength     = 1.12^(rank − 1) × (1.15 if crowned)               multiplies the kind's mult
rider chance = min(0.65, 0.20 + 0.05 × rank)                     1.0 for Sting and for Coil's root
soul cost    = max_soul × (0.04 + 0.005 × rank) × (1 − 0.05 × (mastery tier − 1))
qi cost      = 0
```

- The art is `damage_type: "soul"` with the element's Dao (`dao` = the element when a Dao of that id exists, else its
  parent's; none for `none`), so a Dao tier adds its 5% and cuts cost as for techniques.
- It gains mastery like a technique (per art id, so a new band of the same species keeps it). The mastery cost cut
  applies to the Soul cost.
- It is fed by the room or a held Sphere of its element (+10%, S12 step 7), scrambled by Qi Deviation, and pressed by a
  stronger Presence, as any technique.
- There is no technique grade on an art; the Techniques page shows its rank chip instead.

*Worked example.* A Spirit Awakening 1 character's Mist Wolf band (rank 6, water, fang): **Mistfang Lunge**, a lunge of
120 and two bites of 0.45–0.55 × 1.762 each, slowing the foe 25% for 2 s on 50% of casts, cooldown 9 s, 7% of max Soul
(11 Soul of 161). It deals about 1,980 to a Level 48 foe of another element (60% of its 3,280 HP, §8), and 30% less
to the Mist Wolves themselves, which resist their own element (`stats.json` `mob.own_element_resistance`): a band is
best turned on other beasts than its own kind.

### 3.9 Full slots

When every slot is full, the Wear dialog asks which band the new one displaces. The old band **scatters**, or, with a
gourd in the bag and the toggle on, goes into it (§3.10). Its art leaves any technique slot it held. A band can also be
bottled from its slot at any time (the slot empties, a gourd is spent). Nothing else removes a band.

### 3.10 The band's gift, bottling and release

- **Gift.** Each worn band adds **+1.5% × rank** to Soul regeneration (`soul_regen`, `pct_add`, source `band:<uid>`):
  a rank-6 band +9%, seven rank-9 bands +94.5%. Arts and the Presence both draw on the Soul, so more bands mean more
  arts cast.
- **Bottling.** A **Spirit-Holding Gourd** (item `spirit_holding_gourd`, stack 20) holds one band: from a light, from a
  slot, or a band displaced. The gourd is spent when filled. A character keeps up to **six** bottled bands; they never
  fade. A bottled band is worn later from the Bands tab with the rules of §3.5–3.7 as they stand then.
- **Release.** From a light or a gourd, *Release* lets the soul go: +2 merit, or +5 for a character holding a vow
  (S48); a released gourd is not returned. The light rises and fades.

## 4. Interactions

### 4.1 Beast cores

- A core (S46) is the beast's body; a band is its soul. One kill rolls each on its own: the core at 2% a rank from rank
  2 (`pet_growth.json` `cores`), the light by §3.3.
- A core of the band's element and a covering tier is a **support** when wearing (§3.5), used up whether the wearing
  holds or fails. This is a new sink beside pets devouring, Beast Fire and the Core Exchange, which are unchanged.
- Bands never eat cores otherwise and never give them.

### 4.2 Spirit animals

- **A tamed beast is a companion; a band is a slain beast's soul.** They never turn into each other. No pet can be
  made into a band: fusion stays the only way a pet gives itself up (S46).
- **Only your own hand.** A kill by a pet, a companion or the Copperjaw swarm leaves no light, though it credits you
  for everything else as before.
- **Kin know.** A character cannot tame a species it wears as a band (`attempt_tame` fails: "It smells its kin's soul
  on you."). Pets already tamed are not affected.
- Command capacity (2 from Spirit Awakening 1), suppression, contracts, eggs and breakthroughs are unchanged.

### 4.3 Bestiary Leaves (V10b)

- A species' leaves let a character seek it without having slain it (the Vigil's knowledge of the beast).
- A species at leaf tier 2 or more (5 leaves) is a support when wearing its band.
- Vigil kills never leave lights: a Vigil is settled on the ledger, not fought (§7.2 of `idle_gathering_design.md`).
- The Codex's Bands tab names a species' art once the species is met or leafed.

### 4.4 The S48 paths

| Path | Rule |
|---|---|
| **The Soul line** | Soul Dao tier 2 or more is a support. An elite or boss that dies within Soul Search's window always leaves its light (§3.3). |
| **The Blood path** | A **Blood Band** (from a `demonic` beast: Green Viper, Mud Hound, Mist Vulture today) is worn freely on the path; off it, wearing one costs 5 alignment toward the demonic side. On the path, a Blood Band's art drinks back at the Blood arts' lifesteal (×2, `paths.blood.blood_art_lifesteal_mult`), and a full blood-essence meter may be spent as a support. |
| **The Buddhist path** | Release gives +5 merit to a vow-keeper (+2 to others), so merit milestones calm the heart sooner. The Mercy vow's fleeing foes escape, so they leave no light. |
| **The Poison path** | An art whose rider is poison counts as a poison art for the Poison Body (`poison_body_active` reads worn arts as well as `techniques_known`). |
| **The Confucian path** | A Confucian cannot wear a Blood Band ("An upright heart will not wear it."). The Sought Beast dying while rooted by Rite-Seal Script always leaves its light. Righteous Qi strikes Hollow and demonic foes 25% harder with arts as with every blow. |

### 4.5 Other systems

- **Presence and the Sphere** (S28): the Presence draws Soul as arts do; a held Sphere feeds arts of its element
  (+10%). The rings show while the Presence is held.
- **Qi Deviation** from any source scrambles arts as it scrambles techniques.
- **Keeping Post** (V10): an art in a technique slot counts toward the Vigil's `K` like a technique. Bands have no
  idle part.
- **Auto-hunt** (S49): the Autopilot stops when a light of the Sought Beast appears.
- **Heavenly tribulation and breakthroughs**: wearing cannot start during either; bands do not change bolts or odds.
- **Beast Kings** (S46): their bands are crowned. The Riverbed Serpent (rank 3) is a crowned band any Spirit Awakening
  cultivator can wear safely; the Thousand-Eye Toad (rank 8, realm 8 + 1) is a Sage's challenge; the Nebula Leviathan
  (rank 9, realm 11 + 1) a Sphere Lord's.
- **Trial Tower, beast trials, the beast arena**: their foes are trials or pets and leave no light.
- **Accounts**: bands belong to one character. Twelve characters wear twelve sets.

### 6.3 The authority: FieldAuthority

**Recommendation: FieldAuthority owns band state.** A band is a cultivator's power, not an animal: it is worn about the
body, soul-bound, costs Soul and shows as a field of light, which is what FieldAuthority already is (Presence with its
Soul upkeep, the Sphere with its circle), and its state sits where theirs does, in `CultivatorState.field_powers`.
PetAuthority owns living animals (`c.pets`), is already one of the largest authorities (1,677 lines), and putting bands there
would blur the rule this design keeps sharpest: a tamed beast and a slain beast's soul are different things. What bands
share with pets (ranks, cores, species) is read through public queries, not owned.

What FieldAuthority adds:

- state: `field_powers.bands` (§6.6); transient `lights` (actor → list, per room) and `wearing` (actor → the channel);
- intents (§6.4), queries (`band_slots`, `band_limit`, `bands_worn`, `bottled`, `lights_of`, `light_near`,
  `wear_preview`, `wears_species`, `band_of_art`), and public commands `apply_band_light(actor_id, def_id, level,
  source)` and `apply_band(actor_id, def_id, level, source)` for tests and rewards;
- subscriptions: `actor_defeated` (lights), `hit_landed` and `player_gravely_wounded` (break a wearing), `room_entered`
  (lights fade), `realm_changed` (no effect on bands; the page redraws);
- `tick`: lights' time, the wearing channel.

Small changes elsewhere, each authority writing only its own state:

| Where | Change |
|---|---|
| `ProgressionAuthority` | A public `apply_qi_deviation(actor_id, cause)`: the status, the Codex entry and the `qi_deviation` event, now with `cause` (`"breakthrough"` or `"band"`); `_maybe_deviate` calls it. `equip_technique` and the slot check on restore accept an art of a worn band (`game.field.band_of_art`). A public `apply_unequip_technique(actor_id, tid)` for a displaced band's art. |
| `CombatAuthority` | `use_technique` for a row with `band`: the Soul cost from `BandRules.art_soul_cost`, the damage mult × `art_strength`, the rider's chance, the Blood Band lifesteal. A technique row's `pull` (new on player rows) reuses the pull that combos and enemy attacks already apply. `poison_body_active` reads worn arts. `ally_hits_enemy` and the swarm pass `by`, and the `actor_defeated` payload gains `captured` when the Taming Cauldron took the beast (World's loot handler erases `CombatAuthority.captured` during the same event, so Field cannot read it afterwards). A public `apply_spend_essence(actor_id, amount)` for the blood-essence support. `refresh_stats` is called by Field after a band changes. |
| `EnemyAuthority` | `defeat(e, killer, by := "self")`; the `actor_defeated` payload gains `by` (`self`, `ally`, `swarm`). |
| `StatRules.rebuild` | `"band:"` joins `PERMANENT_PREFIXES`; each worn band adds its Soul regeneration gift. |
| `PetAuthority` | `attempt_tame` fails for a species `game.field.wears_species` reports. |
| `WorldAuthority` | `query_context` offers *Wear* for a light in reach (`game.field.light_near`), ranked like an object. |
| `QuestAuthority` | The `use_technique` objective gains a `band: true` filter. |
| `RelationsAuthority` | none: Field calls the existing `apply_karma` (merit) and `apply_alignment`. |
| `PostAuthority` | none. |

`FieldAuthority`'s header comment and `architecture.md`'s authority table (Field row, and its Single Player / Online
row) gain the bands.

### 6.9 Tests

**`rules_tests` `band_suite`:**

1. **Slots:** `slots_for` at every row and one step below it (Spirit Awakening 4 → 1, Spirit Awakening 5 → 2, Heaven
   Glimpse 1 → 3 … Monarch 1 → 9, Inner Heaven 1 → 9); 0 without the unlock.
2. **Limit:** a Spirit Awakening 1 character against Levels 48 (over 0), 60 (over 1) and 96 (over 5, refused); a
   crowned band +1; Gold Body −1; a false realm ignored.
3. **Risk:** over 0 is certain and uses no supports; over 1 bare is High; two supports make it Low; a third support
   does not count; an injury and Unstable each add a step; heart demon 25 adds one; over 3 bare is Severe and High with
   two supports; over 4 is refused. Each core rule: wrong element, too low a tier, a sub-element's parent.
4. **Lights:** with a fixed seed, 2,000 sought normal kills leave 100 ± 30 lights; unsought normals none; a boss and a
   Beast King always; Soul Search always; none for a hollowed beast, an event beast, a summoned add, a pet's or the
   swarm's kill, a captured beast, or a Vigil; a light fades at 120 s and on leaving the room.
5. **Wearing:** the channel lasts 6 + 2 × rank; half the Soul is spent; a blow breaks it and the light stays; a certain
   wearing lands in the first empty slot; `band_worn` and `system_used` are emitted; Soul regeneration rises by the
   gift after `refresh_stats`.
6. **Failure:** under a seed that fails, Qi Deviation is on and `qi_deviation` comes from Progression with
   `cause: "band"`; the meridian tear's severity equals over; at Severe, HP is a tenth of max and a soul injury is on;
   the band is gone and the cores spent.
7. **Arts:** the art is equippable only while its band is worn and leaves its slot when the band is displaced or
   bottled; its Soul cost is `max_soul × (0.04 + 0.005 × rank)`; a fixed-seed hit of a rank-9 art is 2.476 times a
   rank-1 art's, and a crowned one 1.15 times more; the rider chance by rank; a poison-rider art opens the Poison Body;
   the art survives a save round-trip in its slot.
8. **Gourds and release:** bottling spends a gourd; a seventh bottled band is refused; a bottled band worn later is
   judged at the realm then; release gives +2 merit, +5 with a vow held.
9. **Paths and pets:** a Confucian cannot wear a Blood Band; off the Blood path it costs 5 alignment; blood essence
   is a support and is emptied; Soul Dao tier 2 is a support; leaf tier 2 is a support and one leaf allows seeking; a
   worn species cannot be tamed.
10. **Save:** a character with worn and bottled bands, a Sought Beast and a tally round-trips; an old save loads with
    none.

**`valley_run`:** in `sec_sa1`, after *A Lake Inside*: accept *A Band of Light*, seek the Mist Wolf, travel to
`mp_misty_slopes`, fight Mist Wolves until a light appears (at most 40 kills; if none has come, the labelled shortcut
`Game.field.apply_band_light(c().id, "mist_wolf", 48, "test_shortcut")`), wear it (certain at rank 6), equip
Mistfang Lunge, cast it once on a wolf, finish the quest. `sec_sa5` checks two slots; `sec_hg1` checks three and that
the Wear preview of a rank-8, realm-8 band reads High without supports.

**`data_validation`:** every `beasts` row is a band-eligible enemy and every eligible enemy has a row; every kind and

## 7. Where the numbers come from

- Ranks and realms: `WorldAuthority.beast_rank` and `ProgressionRules.realm_index_for_level`, unchanged.
- Risk words and chances: `curves.json` `risk_words` and `risk_success`, and `ProgressionRules.risk_index`, unchanged.
- Failure: `stats.json` `qi_deviation` (600 s) and `injuries.json`, unchanged; the burst's tenth is the tribulation's.
- Art strength: `1.12^(rank − 1)`, so each rank over is worth 12%, enough to tempt and small enough that a band at the
  limit is never useless.
- Soul costs are a share of max Soul because the Soul pool grows about twenty-fold from Spirit Awakening to Sphere Lord
  (`stats.json` `pools.soul`: 100 at Level 46, 1,650 at Level 97, before Spirit); a flat cost would be heavy at the
  first band and free by the seventh.

## 10. Deliberate differences from the source

- **Rank, not years.** The game's `beast_rank` 1–9 is the age; there is no year count and no tier above 9.
- **A limit by realm, not a table per slot.** The source's own story overturns its per-slot table; Jade River keeps the
  personal limit (your realm, raised by Gold Body) and moves it with preparation instead.
- **Chosen, not rolled at random.** Only the Sought Beast (and Kings, bosses and soul-searched elites) leaves a light,
  so bands are hunted, not dropped on every kill.
- **Failure hurts but does not kill.** Qi Deviation, a meridian tear and a burst to a tenth of HP; the band is lost,
  the character is not.
- **Replaceable, at a price.** A band can be displaced or bottled; the source's rings are for life.
- **One art per band at every rank.** No double arts for the highest tiers; a crowned band is stronger instead.
- **Arts cost Soul by share.** So the first band and the ninth weigh the same on the bar.
- **No bones, no martial soul, no sacrifice.** Cores, Daos and spirit animals already fill those places.
- **Concentric rings at the waist, in the grade ladder's colours.** Not a column of rings up the body, and not the
  source's colour order.

## 11. Decisions taken

Four questions were left open when this page was written. Each takes the recommended answer below, which the user can
overturn before P8b is built:

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| A Band Burst at Severe risk: a tenth of HP, or a full defeat? | A tenth of HP, as a failed tribulation does: failure hurts but never kills |
| Beast art power: 60–90% of the realm's best comparable technique, paid in Soul instead of Qi | Kept; re-checked with `balance_sim` once v1.3's beasts exist |
| The Gravity Golem counts as a beast only through a default in `tools/data/enemies.py` | It becomes a construct in P8b, which also removes its beast cores and rank badge |
| Band colours: by level (the grade dropped at that level) or the grade colours in order | By level, so a band matches the gear its hunting ground drops |
