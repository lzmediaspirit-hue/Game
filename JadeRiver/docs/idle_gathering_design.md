# V10 · Keeping Post — idle gathering (design and build brief)

This page is both the design of Jade River's idle gathering system and the brief a builder (human or agent)
follows to build it. It carries IdleOn's AFK gathering over mechanic for mechanic into Jade River's world and
names. The research behind it, with sources and confidence labels, is `docs/research/idle_gathering_research.md`.
IdleOn is the reference for mechanics only: no name, item, text or art of it appears in the game.

---

## 0. The brief

**Role.** You are building one milestone of a Godot 4 offline-first game whose simulation is split into pure
rules (`scripts/simulation/rules/`) and authorities that own state and emit events
(`scripts/simulation/authority/`), with all content generated from `tools/data/*.py` into `data/*.json`.

**Goal.** Every character on the account who is not being played keeps working. You leave a character at an
ore vein, a herb patch, a fishing spot, an insect swarm or a hunting ground, play someone else, and when you come
back that character has gathered, levelled a craft and filled a pouch at the rates the rules below give. The
player's game becomes a daily round: settle each character's haul, upgrade tools and pouches, move characters to
nodes they can now work, and feed the account-wide systems the haul pays for.

**Definition of done for each phase (V10a–V10d):**

1. Data in `tools/data/posts.py` (and the modules it touches), generated by `build_data.py`, validated by
   `data_validation`.
2. Pure formulas in `PostRules`; state and intents in `PostAuthority`; every new event in `contract.py`.
3. UI reachable on a phone: Roll-Call page, Return Ledger popup, node plates, Post Info.
4. Rules tests for every formula (numbers checked against the worked examples in §3), a settle test, a save
   round-trip, a clock-safety test and a `balance_sim` check of the calibration targets in §8.
5. Full test suite green, `docs/CHANGELOG.md` entry, commit and push.

**Constraints.**

- Names are Jade River's own (the table in §1). Never an IdleOn name.
- Authorities emit only their own events. Randomness goes through `Rng` with a seed derived from the character
  and the settle, so a settle replays identically.
- Nothing is lost silently. Where IdleOn drops overflow on the ground, Jade River stops filling the pouch and
  says when it filled (§3.7).
- New items and animations are reviewed in all poses, facings and dyes (AGENTS.md). New NPCs use existing avatar
  parts and dyes. Icons and props come from the art pipelines (`tools/icons`, `tools/props`).
- The existing idle tasks keep working: Rest, Seclusion and Train are untouched; Hunt and Gather become the
  Vigil and craft posts, and old saves migrate (§5.5).

**Order of work inside a phase:** data → rules → authority → UI → tests → docs.

## 2. The loop

1. **Take a post.** At a gathering node the interact menu offers *Keep post here* when the character knows the
   node's craft and meets its level gate. In a room whose `idle` list has `hunt`, the room menu offers *Keep
   vigil here*. Taking a post opens the Roll-Call to choose who to play next: a post is a place a character may
   be switched out from, like a town.
2. **Everyone works.** Every character with a post accrues from the moment it was taken. The one being played
   does not accrue (it is playing), but its active harvesting trains its crafts at the same EXP per success.
3. **Come back.** Entering a character settles its post: the Return Ledger shows the hours, Diligence, EXP and
   levels, and the haul, with *Send to Storehouse* (default) or *Keep in pouch*. The character is standing at
   its post and keeps it until it walks away or takes another.
4. **Settle all.** The Roll-Call settles every posted character at once into the Storehouse without switching,
   and shows who is full, who ran out of provisions and whose snares are done.
5. **Spend.** Tools, pouches, Seal Scripts, Formation Flags, the Calcination Furnace and the forge and pill
   recipes consume the haul, which raises Finesse, Diligence and capacity, which moves characters to tougher
   nodes.

## 3. Rules (PostRules)

All constants live in `data/posts.json` under `rules`; the values here are the defaults.

### 3.1 Diligence

```
CraftDiligence   = max(0.01, 0.52 + (craft_sources + all_sources) / 100) × deep_multi
MartialDiligence = max(0.01, 0.40 + (martial_sources + all_sources) / 100) × deep_multi
deep_multi = Π (1 + m_i / 100)      (a separate multiplicative layer: Formation Flag "Deep" variants, rare gear)
```

The sources are additive percentages (§6). Late in the game Diligence passes 100%.

### 3.2 Craft levels

Each character has a level and EXP per craft (levels 1–200). EXP to the next level:

```
xp_to_next(L) = floor((15 + L² + 15·L) × (1.225 − min(0.164, 0.135·L / (L + 50)))^L − 30)
```

This gives 7 at L1, about 1,650 at L10, about 170,000 at L30 and about 4.9 million at L50. A settle applies
the EXP after the fact at the rate in force, and levels up as often as it fills.

### 3.3 Finesse

For a craft with attribute A (the character's current attribute value), tool power P, craft level L:

```
base    = 2·P + 4 + stele_power
Finesse = 12 + (base^1.3 + (A + 1)^0.6 + seal_flat)
               × (1 + L / 200)
               × (1 + (A / 100)^0.35)
               × (1 + base / 100)
               × Π (1 + g_i / 100)             groups: Post Arts, Bestiary Leaves, gear, Seal %, Flags, sect
```

| Craft | Attribute | Tool | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vein Delving | Body | delving pick | `mining` (exists) |
| Spirit Foraging | Insight | foraging sickle | `herb_gathering` (exists) |
| River Angling | Body | angling rod | `fishing` (exists) |
| Insect Netting | Agility | silk net | `insect_netting` (new, V10a) |
| Beast Snaring | Agility | snare kit | `beast_snaring` (new, V10c) |
| Ancestral Rites | Spirit | rite tablet | `ancestral_rites` (new, V10c) |

Worked example (a test): an old pick of power 6, attribute 10, level 1, no bonuses: base 16, Finesse ≈ **81**.

### 3.4 Yield of one action

For a node output of Toughness T:

```
r         = Finesse / (10 · T)
chance    = 0                  if r < 0.025
          = min(1, r^0.4)      otherwise                         (the Chance bar; full at Finesse = 10·T)
abundance = r ≥ 1 ? max(1, floor(r^(0.25 + Flow))) : 1          (the Abundance bar; Flow ≤ 0.10)
windfall  = 1 + w + w² + w³ + w⁴                                (w = Windfall chance, chains up to 4 extra)
```

The Chance bar shows `chance`; once it is full the Abundance bar shows progress to the next whole unit,
`frac(r^(0.25 + Flow))`, and "next Abundance at Finesse X".

### 3.5 Actions per hour

```
swing_s   = 6 × (1 + (10 − tool_speed) / 5) / (1 + speed_pct / 100)
actions/h = 3600 / swing_s
```

Tool speed runs 3–10. The factor 6 is Jade River's calibration (a swing is a whole extraction, the same as one
harvest by hand), so post yields sit near half of active harvesting as they do in IdleOn. Node EXP values are
set for this pace (§4).

### 3.6 Rates

```
items/h(output) = actions/h × share × chance × abundance × windfall × Diligence
exp/h           = Σ actions/h × share × chance × exp(output) × exp_mult × Diligence
```

`share` is the output's weight among the node's outputs (1 for a single-output vein; fish spots and swarms use
their weights; night-only fish count a third of the time). Foraging also yields spirit wood on 1 success in 20
as a side drop.

### 3.7 Pouches and the Storehouse

- Categories: **ore, herb, fish, insect, material, critter, wisp**. Each character's pouch has 4 compartments
  per category; capacity(category) = 4 × compartment_cap × Π(1 + capacity_pct / 100), hard cap 2.05 × 10⁹.
- Compartment cap: base **10**; sewing tiers **25 · 50 · 100 · 250 · 500 · 1,000 · 2,000 · 5,000 · 10,000 ·
  20,000 · 25,000 · 30,000 · 35,000**. A tier is sewn per character and category by Tailor Xun in Stoneford
  (taels and materials); the highest applies.
- A post fills the pouch; when a category is full, items stop and EXP continues. The ledger says "full since
  7 h 12 m". Nothing overflows.
- The **Storehouse** is account-wide: an item → count table (per item up to 2.05 × 10⁹, no slots). *Send to
  Storehouse* empties the pouch into it; *Withdraw* moves up to a bag's worth into the bag in any town. Seal
  Scripts, Flags, the Furnace and tool forging draw on it directly.

### 3.8 Settling

```
hours     = clamp((now − post.since) / 3600, 0, 24 × 90)          (Clock.elapsed_since; a clock moved back
                                                                    resets `since` and settles nothing)
fill_h(c) = (capacity(c) − held(c)) / items_per_hour(c)            per category
items     = rate × min(hours, fill_h)                              then a seeded draw: floor(x) + (rng < frac(x))
exp       = exp_rate × hours
```

Rates are taken at settle time, as IdleOn does: an upgrade made while playing someone else counts for the whole
absence. A settle with nothing to add is silent. No general time cap applies to posts: the pouch is the cap.
(Seclusion's realm progress keeps its own 12-hour cap from S23.)

### 3.9 Hour Incense

Burned on a posted character from the Roll-Call, a stick grants that many hours of its post at its current
rates at once (items into the pouch, capacity applies; EXP). Sticks of **1, 2, 4, 12, 24 and 72 hours**;
**Wandering Incense** grants a seeded random 5–500 hours (median near 24). Incense hours do not level Formation
Flags. Sources: activity chests, the Trial Tower, the auction, Rites.

## 5. Architecture

### 5.1 State

On the character (`GameCharacter.posts`, saved):

```
posts = {
  "post": {"kind": "craft"|"vigil"|"snare"|"rite", "craft": "delving", "room": "...", "object": "...",
           "since": utc, "settled": utc},
  "crafts": {"delving": {"xp": 0.0}, ...},           # level from xp_to_next
  "tools": {"delving": "old_pickaxe", ...},          # equipped per craft (tool belt)
  "pouch": {"ore": {"tier": 0, "items": {"copper_ore": 12}}, ...},
  "arts": {"dreaming_artisan": 3, ...},              # V10d
  "provisions": {"item": "jade_carp_congee", "count": 20},   # V10b
  "bench": {...}                                     # V10c
}
```

On the account (`AccountState`): `storehouse` (item → count), `seals`, `steles`, `flags`, `mirror`,
`furnace`, `favours`, `leaves` (V10b–d).

### 5.2 Authority and rules

- `PostRules` (pure): `xp_to_next`, `level_for`, `finesse`, `yield_of`, `swing_seconds`, `rates`,
  `capacity`, `fill_hours`, `settle_amounts`, `diligence`, and in V10b `kills_per_hour`, `hit_chance`,
  `sweep_tier`, `survivability`.
- `PostAuthority` (state and events). Intents: `take_post`, `leave_post`, `settle_post`, `settle_all`,
  `send_to_storehouse`, `withdraw_storehouse`, `equip_tool`, `sew_pouch`, `burn_incense`; later
  `set_provisions`, `set_snare`, `collect_snare`, `hold_rite`, `bench_assign`, `mirror_sample`,
  `furnace_toggle`, `furnace_refine`, `level_seal`, `plant_flag`, `learn_art`, `buy_favour`.
- The account authority's `enter_character` calls `PostAuthority.settle_post` and returns the ledger as
  `welcome` (the welcome page becomes the Return Ledger). `switch_character` also allows a switch when the
  active character has just taken a post.
- Active harvesting (`WorldAuthority` harvest) calls `PostAuthority.apply_craft_xp` for the node's craft.

### 5.3 Events (contract)

`post_taken`, `post_left`, `post_settled` {character, hours, items, exp, levels, full}, `craft_leveled`
{actor, craft, level}, `pouch_sewn`, `storehouse_changed`, `incense_burned`; V10b `vigil_settled`,
`leaf_found`; V10c `snare_set`, `snare_collected`, `rite_held`, `bench_collected`; V10d `seal_leveled`,
`stele_leveled`, `flag_planted`, `flag_leveled`, `art_learned`, `favour_bought`, `mirror_printed`,
`salt_refined`. Quest kinds: `reach_craft_level`, `settle_hours`, `use_system` "post".

### 5.4 Data

`data/posts.json`: `rules` (every constant in §3 and §7), `crafts`, `nodes` (output → toughness, exp, gate,
category), `pouch_tiers`, `sewing` (costs per tier), `incense`. Tools are items (`type: tool`, `post: {craft,
power, speed, finesse_pct, level_req}`). Insects are items (`type: insect`). Swarm objects are room objects.

### 5.5 Migration

A save with an idle task `gather` becomes a craft post at the first matching node in that room; `hunt` becomes a
Vigil in that room. Their `started_utc` becomes `since`. Characters gain level 1 in every craft they have the
unlock for.

## 6. Where Diligence, Finesse and capacity come from

Every source uses one of two curves: `add` (x₁·L) or `decay` (x₁·L / (L + x₂)).

| Source | Phase | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Tools | V10a | power and speed by tier (§7.1); late tiers add Finesse % |
| Craft level | V10a | +0.5% Finesse a level |
| Pouch sewing | V10a | capacity tier |
| Sect (existing `idle_rate_bonus`) | V10a | +Diligence % from the sect's buildings |
| Provisions | V10b | Vigil survivability |
| Bestiary Leaves | V10b | per species: +Finesse %, +Diligence %, +drop rate, by leaf tier (1, 5, 25, 100 copies) |
| Post Vows | V10c | boon and curse, e.g. *Vow of the Short Lamp*: +25% craft EXP, posts stop after 10 h |
| Post Arts | V10d | *Dreaming Artisan* 20·L/(L+40) Craft Diligence; *Sleeping Sword* 20·L/(L+50) Martial; *Water-Clock Breath* 8·L/(L+50) both; *Steady Hand* Finesse %; *Deep Sleeves* capacity %; *Windfall Knack* w; *Flowing Hand* Flow ≤ 0.05; *Echo Sampling* (Mirror) |
| Seal Scripts | V10d | account-wide flat Finesse per craft, capacity %, Diligence %; damped for a character whose craft level is under the seal's |
| Guardian Steles | V10d | +0.3 tool power a level per craft |
| Formation Flags | V10d | in the room planted: +1% Diligence +0.1% a level; *Deep* flag variant ×(1 + 2%) |
| Magistrate's Favours | V10d | +5% Martial, +3% Craft Diligence; a 2.2% chance of double EXP on a ledger |

Rough targets: a new character 52% / 40%; mid Expanse 80–120%; late Act III past 100%.

### 7.2 Vigil (V10b)

```
spawn_cap/h  = 3600 × mobs / (respawn_s + 0.1)
fighter_cap/h = 3600 × K / (walk_s + wait_s × max((hp / avg_hit + 0.52) / hit, 1)) × pace     K ∈ [1, 2.2]
kills/h      = floor(min(spawn_cap, fighter_cap)) × MartialDiligence × sweep × alive
sweep        = max(1, tier × 0.5)          tier = floor(log2(max_hit / hp)), from twice the HP
```

- `hit`, `avg_hit` and `max_hit` come from Jade River's own S12 pipeline: 48 seeded `CombatRules.resolve` blows
  (a fixed `Rng.keyed` estimate) against each of the room's spawns at the middle of its level band, weighted by
  how many of each the room holds. The Vigil agrees with the fight you would have.
- `walk_s` is the room's width shared among its spawns over the walking speed (205, + Move Speed); `wait_s` is the
  weapon family's `hits_per_s` with Attack Speed. `K` = 1 + 0.12 per technique slotted, at most 2.2.
- `pace` = 0.35 scales the fighter's cap to Jade River's fights, which are slower than IdleOn's (wind-ups, dodges,
  blows taken). Most valley rooms are held back by their spawn cap, as IdleOn's early maps are.
- **Survivability:** the foes' blows (the same S12 pipeline the other way, one every 2.2 s while fighting) against
  HP regeneration and **Provisions**: the healing food in the character's bag, the one that heals most per bite,
  eaten as damage outpaces regeneration. Without food the fighter falls every max HP / net damage hours and loses
  600 s each time.
- Each kill rolls the room's loot at expected value × drop rate (40 seeded rolls, scaled; the pouch still caps it),
  pays its coins, gives a quarter of a hunted kill's realm progress (S23 keeps progress away slower than play), and
  counts for **Bestiary Leaves**: one leaf in 1,000 kills of a species, account-wide. A species' leaves reach tiers
  at 1, 5, 25 and 100 and give +1 / 2 / 3 / 5% of one bonus fixed per species: Martial Diligence, Craft Diligence,
  Finesse, pouch capacity or drop rate.
- "Keep vigil here" is on the Roll-Call in any room whose `idle` list has `hunt`. An old idle Hunt task becomes a
  Vigil.

### 7.3 Timed stations (V10c)

- **Beast Snaring.** Snares set on beast trails (room objects in hunting rooms). Kit tiers unlock durations:
  kit 0: 20 min → 1 critter / 1 EXP, 1 h → 2/2, 8 h → 10/8, 20 h → 20/15; kit 1 adds 40 h → 35/50 and a
  *radiant* chance; kit 2 adds 3 h → 5/5, 60 h → 50/40, 120 h → 100/80 and all-critter or all-EXP variants; the
  top kit reaches 28 days → 550/1,150. Short snares pay more an hour, long ones more a visit. Critters (jade
  frog, mist hare, reed ferret, cloud marmot, thunder hedgehog, frost stoat, sand fox, star gecko) need Finesse
  ≥ their Toughness. *Hunter's Recall* (a Post Art) collects remotely at 50%.
- **Ancestral Rites.** At a sect's ancestral altar a character builds **rite charge**:
  `charge/h = 6 / max(5.7 − 0.2 · tablet_speed^1.3 − L/40, 0.57)`, up to a cap (50 + tablet tier × 25). The
  active character spends charge on an **altar defence** (an auto-resolved wave fight from its stats) for
  **Spirit Wisps** = `5 × (1 + floor(100 · (F / (10 · T))^0.25) / 100) × ((5 + wave) / 10)^2.6`. Wisps pay for
  Post Vows.
- **Apprentice Bench.** Each character's bench has 1–3 apprentices making components with no input:
  `items/h = 3600 × speed / progress`; capacity = material compartment × (2 + 0.1 × capacity points); points
  from Snaring/Rites levels split among speed, capacity and EXP. Components (hemp cord 100, bronze rivets 200,
  kiln brick 350, lacquer 700, whetstone 1,200, spirit glue 2,000) feed tool forging and pouch sewing.

### 7.4 Account systems (V10d)

- **Mirror of Echoes** (sect building). A character with *Echo Sampling* (10% + 0.075% a level) inscribes a
  share of its current post's item rate into one of two mirror slots; every hour, offline, the mirror adds that
  much to the Storehouse, whatever the character does next.
- **Calcination Furnace** (Formation Guild). Salt lines burn Storehouse inputs every cycle (15 min for salts
  1–3, 1 h for 4–6, 100 h beyond): cost `floor(rank^1.5) × qty`, fire `floor(rank^1.3)`; *Refine* turns fire
  into **Essence Salts** and ranks the line up. Salts pay for Seal Scripts past level 10, Flag upgrades and tool
  tiers from 6 up.
- **Seal Scripts**, **Guardian Steles**, **Formation Flags**, **Post Arts**, **Magistrate's Favours**: §6.
- QoL: *Auto-Settle* (the ledger skips its popup and sends to the Storehouse), *Storehouse Seal* (a post's main
  output goes straight to the Storehouse), remote collect for snares and benches.

## 10. Deliberate differences from IdleOn

- **No lost loot.** Pouches stop filling at capacity and say when; IdleOn drops overflow on the ground.
- **Roll-Call settle all.** IdleOn settles one character per switch; Jade River also settles everyone at once.
- **Swing time ×6** and matching node EXP, so posts sit beside Jade River's hand harvesting.
- **Vigil uses Jade River's combat numbers** (hit, damage) inside IdleOn's kills-per-hour shape.
- **Pouches are sewn, not found.** One tailor sews tiers instead of 91 pouch items.
- **Posts never cap by time.** Seclusion's realm progress keeps its 12-hour cap.
