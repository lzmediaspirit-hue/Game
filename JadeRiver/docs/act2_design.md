# Act II · The Azure Expanse (v1.1) — design

The build prompt gives Act II in outline (zone tier 2, levels 55–81, ceiling Sage Sovereign 3,
Storm Ward attunement 10 → 60, twelve monsters, two bosses and a list of systems). This page fills
in the geography, the story and the order in which the systems unlock, so each build phase adds a
playable, tested slice. Names are original to Jade River.

## Premise

Past the Ascension Gate the valley's sky opens onto the Azure Expanse: storm plains, frozen heights
and a mirror lake under floating peaks, held together by the **Nine Peaks Alliance**, a league of
nine sects that taxes every sky road. Independent cultivators and the old clans live in its margins.

Three threads carry the act:

1. **The Grey Pilgrim.** A robed stranger who never casts a shadow is buying Hollow shards across the
   Expanse. He serves the man from the Cleansing vision, Yan Heng. He wants the seal the Tomb King of
   Sunscar still holds.
2. **Elder Gu.** The smuggler who fled with his ledger (Act I) has sold it to Starsea pirates, and
   the ledger names every valley family that ever paid him. He resurfaces at the Skyport Wreck.
3. **Lu's crossing.** Lu came through the Expanse long ago. Five journal pages mark his road; the last
   shows that he, too, once stood before the Tomb King.

## Build phases

- **A · Foundation (done).** Zone-aware map; the crossing from the Ascension Gate; Cloudgate Port and the
  Thunderhorn Plains; Spark Weasel and Thunderhorn Rhino; Storm Ward jades and shards; chapter 11.
- **B · Heights and lake.** Rimefrost Heights, Mirrorwater Lake, four monsters, the Thousand-Eye Toad;
  chapter 12.
- **C · Nine Peaks.** Alliance or independence, auction house, clans (Ironroot), Gale Canyons;
  chapter 13.
- **D · Sunscar.** Desert, the Tomb of Sunscar and its King; chapter 14.
- **E · Starsea.** Skyport Wreck, pirates, star charts and vessels, sect war; chapters 15–16 and the
  Presence Trial.

## Attunement numbers

Four jades per zone; raising a jade from level n to n + 1 costs n + 1 of the zone's shards, up to 15, so
one jade costs 120 shards and a full set 480 (Storm Ward 60, the Skyport Wreck's need). Rooms ask for
their region's value unless they set their own (the Stormgrass Verge asks 6, the Lightning Scar 12);
safe rooms ask nothing. Spark Weasels drop a shard about one kill in three, Thunderhorn Rhinos one or two
in half their kills, and jars and chests of the Expanse carry them too.
