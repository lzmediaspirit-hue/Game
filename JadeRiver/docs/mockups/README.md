# P3 mockups

Full-screen 1280 × 720 mockups of Jade River's HUD, hub and system pages, for approval before any restyle is built
(phase P3 of `docs/roadmap_master_ui.md`, rows U9–U10). Each is drawn in the build's own fonts, colours, HD frames and
art, with a real progressed character (Tester, the valley_run character; the checkpoint is named in each row) and real
data. Sources are in `src/`, the shared kit in `kit/` (see `kit/README.md`), generated figures and world plates in
`assets/`. Render with `python3 tools/dev/render_mockups.py`.

References are to `docs/research/ui_reference_notes.md` (section and the source numbers there),
`docs/research/retention_notes.md` and `docs/review-v12.md`. The game never names a reference.

## System pages (A)

Seven system pages, each a themed redesign of today's page that keeps everything the page does
(`docs/ui_inventory.md` §2.2). Numbers come from the valley_run character's checkpoints as saved (snapshots of `ls6_end`,
`bf2` and `ae_end` taken on 2026-09-26, read from the save and from a capture of the real page on that save); the
Level shown is `ProgressionRules.level` (98 at `ls6_end`; 01 and 02 print the stage's first Level, 97, see B15). Page
slots are 76 px with the 64 px icon at 1:1 (the icon study's decision, `kit.css` `.k-slot76`); the one study item that
appears, the Healing Pill (07), is drawn in Style A, every other icon is today's. Figures are the real sprite layers
composed by `render_mockups.py figure` from the checkpoint's outfit, scaled 3x with nearest-neighbour; the Bag and the
Character page show the figure wearing what is worn with the worn slots round it.

### What the system pages (A) ask the user to approve

- **06**: the Combat tab becomes the Star Chart and shows every technique, the unlearned as dim stars; the constellations
  by element with the families as paths; the sect tree also drawn on this page (it stays on Sect › Role); the loadout as
  two rings of four at 76 px; learned stars at 48 px (the icon study renders techniques natively at 48).
- **07, 08**: slot rims by grade for every item, with quality as a corner gem on gear; kind filters; the grid shows four
  rows of 76 px slots and scrolls; the next gourd's slots shown locked; what is carried to the HUD as a text summary
  under the doll; Self-detonate, Appraise and the rarer actions in the "···" menu; the hint card on an empty bag.
- **09**: the Stats and Titles tabs fold into the Overview; stats shown in `stats.json`'s formats, with Soul attack,
  Soul defence and Will added; the origin's name and one-line description (new strings for `data/origins.json`, B10).
- **10**: Care and Growth become one Stable leaf; the Swarm keeps its tab and gets a card on the Stable.
- **11**: Buildings and Disciples become one Courtyard; disciples drawn where they are and candidates at the gate; a
  blocked Build offers Open storage.
- **12**: Main, Side and Daily become one Current tab beside Done; side quests grouped by region; the direction line
  drawn as a route with Go.

Open items found while drawing (A): a sect building takes its materials from the bag only, so the Treasury is blocked
while 49 Copper Ore sit in storage; four sect buildings (Herb Terraces, Mirror of Echoes, Ancestral Shrine, Expanse
Outpost) have no place in `hv_sect_grounds`; the Treasury's output key is `taels_per_level` but it sizes the storage
chest; the Copperjaw swarm has no creature art (the card shows the Copperjaw Box); technique sources (`library_1`,
`night_peddler` …) have no player-facing names, which an unlearned star's line needs; `stats.json` gives `move_speed`
the percent format while the page shows the speed (242); origins have no name or description strings (B10); valley_run
re-makes the checkpoints while agents work, so each mockup names the checkpoint its numbers come from.

`monarch` and `inner_heaven` have no colour in `data/grades.json`; page tabs are drawn 40 px tall in the build (their tap
area is 48 since P4a; the kit draws them 48).
