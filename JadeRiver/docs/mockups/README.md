# P3 mockups

Full-screen 1280 × 720 mockups of Jade River's HUD, hub and system pages, for approval before any restyle is built
(phase P3 of `docs/roadmap_master_ui.md`, rows U9–U10). Each is drawn in the build's own fonts, colours, HD frames and
art, with a real progressed character (Tester, the valley_run character; the checkpoint is named in each row) and real
data. Sources are in `src/`, the shared kit in `kit/` (see `kit/README.md`), generated figures and world plates in
`assets/`. Render with `python3 tools/dev/render_mockups.py`.

References are to `docs/research/ui_reference_notes.md` (section and the source numbers there),
`docs/research/retention_notes.md` and `docs/review-v12.md`. The game never names a reference.

## What each mockup asks the user to approve

- **01, 02**: techniques and consumables fold away when no foe is near; the boss carries no world label (its bar names
  it) and wears the skull mark; companions show only an HP line in a fight; the currency pill rests in boss arenas; the
  system log moves above the joystick zone.
- **03**: the grouping and names of the five bays; one line of state under a tablet only when something waits.
- **04**: the Overview becomes the ascent; the method, energy and stability facts shrink to one line (their detail stays
  on Foundation and Methods).
- **05**: the sequence's timing.

Open items found while drawing: Companions and Characters share the `characters` glyph in the hub; the grades `law`,
`monarch` and `inner_heaven` have no colour in `data/grades.json`; page tabs are drawn 40 px tall in the build (their tap
area is 48 since P4a; the kit draws them 48).

## System pages (B)

Drawn after 00–05 were approved, so they follow its two notes: no portrait roundel (the HUD's player panel in 21 is the
name, the realm and three full-width bars), and the approved icon style A wherever one of the twelve study icons appears
(`icon_study/icons/A_*.png`: the healing pill, the star lotus and the empty-slot cloud seal as 64 px icons 1:1 in
76 px page slots; the healing pill and the inner-art manual rendered natively at 32 px for rows, in
`assets/iconA32_*.png`). Every other icon is today's, drawn at 1x or 2x. None of these pages is the Bag or the Character
page, so the note about showing the character's full figure in the equipped gear does not apply to them; the shop's bag
column shows only what can be sold.

  full.
- **14**: tiles in place of the Works tabs; the totals panel; a locked tile names the offered quest and its giver

Open items found while drawing (B):

- The hub's Works tablet (03, qu5) says "After Keeping Post", but at qu5 Keeping Post is done and Works waits on Elder
  Hu's An Idle Art; left as approved, for the locked-text change above.
- Buy-back keeps the last 20 sales with no daily expiry (`economy_authority.gd` `sell`), while its empty string says
  "Nothing sold today."; 17 and 20 follow the code.
- A completed Collection page grants no stat today (only the `collection_page_completed` event), so Seal I's stat line
  in 18 is a proposal, like Seal II's.
