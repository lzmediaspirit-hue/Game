# P3 mockups

Full-screen 1280 × 720 mockups of Jade River's HUD, hub and system pages, for approval before any restyle is built
(phase P3 of `docs/roadmap_master_ui.md`, rows U9–U10). Each is drawn in the build's own fonts, colours, HD frames and
art, with a real progressed character (Tester, the valley_run character; the checkpoint is named in each row) and real
data. Sources are in `src/`, the shared kit in `kit/` (see `kit/README.md`), generated figures and world plates in
`assets/`. Render with `python3 tools/dev/render_mockups.py`. A page that carries `<meta name="mockup-check">` is also
measured in the browser after it renders (the Bag concepts, the world map, the Calendar): the render fails if two text runs
touch, if text touches a node, a mark or a card it is not part of, if text is under 14 px or clipped, or if a button is
under 48 px; `render_mockups.py --check <name>` runs the measure alone.

References are to `docs/research/ui_reference_notes.md` (section and the source numbers there),
`docs/research/retention_notes.md` and `docs/review-v12.md`. The game never names a reference.

(decision 18), secret and lost arts stay unknown until they are found (decision 19), and the Works objects are drawn
better (decision 21). From the reference the pages take its structure and its feeling: a chooser at the left with a
mastery bar for each weapon family and the character standing under them; a tall centred tree of illustrated node cards

no drawing. The names are `docs/technique_plan.md`'s (the Water tree's free hand, its path arts and its Act I keystone),
the pictures are composed from the game's own parts, and the page keeps its identity (decision 14): the element seals
along the top, the loadout dock along the foot and, under the tree, the element's own chart (Water's soundings and
currents). The Lost Arts tab is the explorer's album that `docs/page_identity.md` §9 chose, with a paper mount at each
corner of a card.

`assets/techart_*.png` at 144 px for the reading): the element's disc colours from the emblem composer as the ground;
